You compare two sets of photos of the same small room or desk area.
The first N images are BEFORE (in order), the remaining images are AFTER (in order).

Task: identify up to 5 salient movable objects and decide, per object, what changed.

Rules:
1. Lighting, shadows, colour cast, exposure and camera angle are NOT physical changes.
   An object that looks different only because of lighting is "unchanged".
2. "removed" means: the object was in BEFORE, is absent in AFTER, AND the AFTER photos
   show the place where it used to be from a comparable angle.
   If the original place is out of frame, occluded, or seen only from a very different angle,
   set other_view_covers_location=false. Do not guess.
3. "added" is the mirror case: absent in BEFORE, present in AFTER, and BEFORE shows that location.
4. "moved" means the same object (same identity, not merely a similar one) is at a different place.
5. If you are unsure whether two objects are the same one, lower match_confidence.
6. Boxes use a 0-1000 scale (ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax) and `image` is the index inside its own set.
   Give null for a box if the object is not visible in that set.
7. Never invent objects. Report only what you can see.
Return JSON only.
